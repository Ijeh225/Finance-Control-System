Now, what I observed from the current user structure is that the Payment Assistants are too limited in access. For example, I noticed that a Payment Assistant cannot create or manage vendors/beneficiaries. That setup is not what we want for this platform.

The main purpose of this system is to reduce stress for the MD, not increase it. The MD should not be responsible for operational tasks such as:

* Creating vendors
* Adding beneficiaries
* Creating expenses
* Uploading invoices
* Scheduling bills
* Managing payment records
* Updating liabilities

All those operational responsibilities should belong to the Payment Assistants.

The MD’s role should mainly be:

* Reviewing scheduled payments
* Viewing pending approvals
* Checking outstanding liabilities
* Reviewing user activities
* Making comments or instructions
* Approving, rejecting, or holding payments

The workflow should be simple for the MD.

For example:
Once the MD comes to the office and logs into the platform, all he should need to do is:

* Navigate to a particular user such as Mr. A, Mr. B, or Mr. C
* Open that user’s pending jobs/payments
* Review the scheduled bills
* Make comments or approvals

Example comments:

* Approved
* Pay ₦50,000 first
* Hold till tomorrow
* Confirm invoice
* Use another wallet/account

Once the MD comments or approves anything, the assigned Payment Assistant should immediately receive a notification on their dashboard.

The Payment Assistants should handle almost all operational and financial preparation work inside the system, including:

* Creating vendors/beneficiaries
* Managing vendor balances
* Creating expenses
* Uploading proofs/documents
* Scheduling payments
* Managing wallets/accounts
* Recording transactions
* Processing partial payments
* Updating liabilities
* Preparing bills for MD review

The MD should function more like an executive reviewer and approval authority, not the person handling day-to-day financial data entry.

Please restructure the permissions and workflow to allocate most operational responsibilities to the Payment Assistants while keeping the MD dashboard focused on review, visibility, approvals, comments, and financial oversight.
