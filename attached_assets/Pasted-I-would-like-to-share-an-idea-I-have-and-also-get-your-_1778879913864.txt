I would like to share an idea I have and also get your professional opinion and modifications on it. What I want to build is a private Executive Treasury and Expense Management Platform specifically for my MD and a few selected staff assisting him with payment transactions. This platform is not meant for the company’s full branch operations; it is strictly a personal financial management and payment control system for my MD and the people he authorizes to use it.

Currently, my MD has about three people assisting him with financial transactions and payments. Let’s call them Mr. A, Mr. B, and Mr. C. Each of these users should have their own login credentials and personal dashboard/workspace inside the platform.

The idea is that each user will manage specific accounts or wallets where money comes in and goes out. Inside the platform, we want to create something similar to an internal banking or wallet system where users can:

* Create and manage bank/wallet accounts
* Add account details
* Perform internal transactions
* Move funds from one wallet/account to another
* Keep complete transaction records and history for every movement of money

The system must record:

* Who initiated the transaction
* Amount moved
* Source and destination account
* Date and time
* Reason/narration
* Approval/comment history

Each user (Mr. A, Mr. B, or Mr. C) should also be able to create expenses or bills directly from their dashboard. While creating an expense, the user should be able to:

* Select the wallet/account to debit from
* Add vendor/beneficiary details
* Add description of work done
* Upload supporting documents or invoices
* Schedule the payment date
* Submit the expense for MD review

One of the most important parts of this system is the beneficiary/vendor running balance structure.

For example:
Today, Lucy performs a job worth ₦100,000.
Tomorrow, Lucy performs another job worth ₦100,000.

Instead of creating isolated expenses, the system should automatically accumulate Lucy’s total outstanding balance and show:

* Total bill: ₦200,000
* Amount paid so far
* Remaining balance

The system must also support partial payments. For example:

* If ₦50,000 is paid today, the system should automatically update Lucy’s outstanding balance to ₦150,000.
* Every payment made should be documented and traceable.

The beneficiary/vendor profile should display:

* Total accumulated bills
* Job descriptions
* Expense history
* Partial payment history
* Outstanding liabilities
* Payment proofs
* MD comments and approvals
* Timeline of activities

Another important feature is scheduled payments and MD review workflow.

The users working under my MD should be able to prepare and arrange bills before the MD resumes work. Once the MD logs into his dashboard, he should be able to:

* Select a particular user such as Mr. A, Mr. B, or Mr. C
* View all scheduled bills/payments for that day
* View tomorrow’s scheduled bills
* Review pending approvals
* See outstanding liabilities and overdue bills

For example:
If the MD clicks on “Mr. A” from the menu, the dashboard should display all of Mr. A’s scheduled payments for that day.

The MD should then be able to:

* Approve
* Reject
* Hold
* Partially approve
* Make comments or instructions directly on the payment

Example comments:

* Pay ₦50,000 first
* Hold till tomorrow
* Confirm invoice
* Use another account
* Approved

Once the MD comments or approves anything, the assigned user should instantly receive a notification on their own dashboard.

I want this platform to feel modern, executive, smart, and financially structured, almost like a private treasury command center. I would like the system to have:

* Fancy and professional dashboard design
* Interactive financial cards
* Smart tables and charts
* Real-time notifications
* Audit trail and activity tracking
* Search and filtering
* Excel and PDF report downloads
* Wallet statements
* Vendor statements
* Scheduled payment reports
* Outstanding liabilities reports
* User activity reports

I would also like the system to be designed from an accounting and treasury management perspective, not just a normal expense tracker. I need help refining the structure, workflow, and financial logic so the platform becomes professional, scalable, and suitable for executive-level financial control.
